About Me

Crafting accurate 3D assets and reliable training data.

Hello! I'm Abid Ahmad, a 3D artist with a passion for transforming ideas into reality through 3D modeling and data annotation. With 4 years of experience across both fields, I'm dedicated to delivering high-quality results that meet your specific needs.

I work with jewelry makers needing production-ready CAD for 3D printing, brands needing photorealistic product renders, and teams needing clean, verified training data. From intricate filigree rings to full product texturing and animation, I take projects from concept to final pixel — and from raw data to verified labels.
